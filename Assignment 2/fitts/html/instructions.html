Instructions

Welcome to George's pointing device evaluation study. You'll be participating in a series of target-clicking tasks that will help us understand how people interact with different input devices. The study will take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.

Study Task

During this study, you will be asked to click on circular targets that appear on the screen. Your goal is to click each target as quickly and accurately as possible. The targets will vary in size and distance. You'll start with a practice session to get familiar with the task, followed by the actual trials.

We will be recording the time it takes you to reach each target and whether your clicks were accurate. This data will help us evaluate the performance characteristics of different pointing devices.
