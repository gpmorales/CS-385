Instructions

This experiment will take approximately 5 minutes to complete. Once all trials are completed, you will be presented with a summary of your performance and the option to download your data as a CSV.

Study Trial Overview

  • Targets: Circular items placed randomly on the screen. Some are green (valid), others are gray (distractors).
  • Selection: Simply move your cursor near a green target. The cursor will grow to help you snap to the nearest item.
  • Click to Confirm: Left-click to select the active target (indicated by the bubble lock).
  • Speed + Accuracy: Try to select the green targets as fast and precisely as possible. Avoid clicking non-green distractors.

Cursor Behavior

  • The bubble radius changes dynamically based on target proximity.
  • The closest target is highlighted automatically.
  • If multiple targets are close, the cursor may split or wobble to indicate ambiguity (in advanced mode).
  • No need to perfectly align — just get close and click!

Data Collected During Trials?

The system will automatically track the following metrics during each trial:

  • Time Taken – How long it takes to complete each selection.
  • Correct Selections – Number of required targets selected.
  • Incorrect Selections – Number of extra/non-targets selected.
  • Deselection Actions – How often users undoes a selection.
  • Overall Accuracy – The percentage of correct selections.
