Instructions

Welcome to my pointing device evaluation study. This study is designed to assess how users interact with a trail-based selection technique. The experiment will take approximately 5 minutes to complete.

Study Objective

During this study, you will be asked to interact with randomly positioned items on the screen using a crossing-based selection technique rather than traditional clicking. The goal is to select required items (HIGHLIGHTED IN YELLOW) while avoiding unnecessary selections. Your speed and accuracy will be measured.

How to Use the Selection Tool

  • Activate the Brush Mode: Press CTRL on your keyboard to activate/deactivate the selection brush. A custom cursor (a white circle with an "X") will appear.
  • Start Selecting Targets: After pressing CTRL once to activate brush mode, press and hold the left mouse button. This will create a selection trail as you move hte mouse around.
  • Selection Delay: A short delay (250ms) is required before a selection is confirmed.
  • Auto-Grouping: Nearby targets may be automatically selected together if they are close enough, simulating real-world pointing behavior.
  • Deselecting Items: If you make an incorrect selection, you can right-click (while in brush mode, so the circle with the X is visible) on the item to deselect it.
  • Advancing Trials: Once you are satisfied with your selections, click the "Next Trial" button to proceed.

Data Collected During Trials?

The system will automatically track the following metrics during each trial:

  • Time Taken – How long it takes to complete the selection.
  • Correct Selections – Number of required targets selected.
  • Incorrect Selections – Number of extra/non-targets selected.
  • Deselection Actions – How often users undo a selection.
  • Overall Accuracy – The percentage of correct selections.

Once all trials are completed, you will be presented with a summary of your performance and the option to download your data as a CSV!.
